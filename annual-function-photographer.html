Photography & events

Why book iCube Media

An annual day is the biggest night of the school year. Parents want to see their child on stage, and the school wants a film that shows its best side. We place multiple cameras so every performance is covered from the front and from the wings.

After the event you get a full-length film, short highlight reels for Instagram, and an edited photo gallery. Drone shots of the campus and the crowd add a cinematic opening.
